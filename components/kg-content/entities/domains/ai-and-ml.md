*AI & ML Productionisation, Reliability & Responsible Controls Authority*

## Purpose

Productionise AI and ML capabilities safely and reliably across Tyro by providing shared lifecycle tooling, operational controls, and governance mechanisms — while keeping business decision authority in relevant domains.

## Category

Data & Intelligence Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Model registry and lineage management; Training pipeline orchestration and CI/CD. Not authoritative for: Fraud/credit/pricing decision policy → Relevant business domains; Operational master truth → Operational domains.
