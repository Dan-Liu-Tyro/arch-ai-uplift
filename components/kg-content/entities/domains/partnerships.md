*Partner Relationship & Commercial Participation Authority*

## Purpose

Maintain the authoritative record and lifecycle for all third-party organisations Tyro engages with as partners, and govern how those partners participate in Tyro’s go-to-market and product ecosystem.

## Category

Business Operations Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Partner lifecycle management; Onboard → activate → suspend → offboard. Not authoritative for: Customer legal entity master & group/franchise hierarchy → Customer & Identity; Lead/opportunity pipeline and sales stages → Customer Acquisition & Growth.
