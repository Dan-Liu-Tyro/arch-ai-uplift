*Merchant Clearing, Net Payout & Settlement Truth Authority*

## Purpose

Aggregate cleared acquiring transactions, apply fees/adjustments, and produce the authoritative calculation of what Tyro owes each merchant (and when), including settlement batch formation and reconciliation controls.

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Settlement batch formation and lifecycle; Define settlement cycles, groupings, cut-offs. Not authoritative for: Transaction truth and clearing state → Payments Processing Domain; Dispute/chargeback case lifecycle and outcomes → Disputes & Chargebacks Domain.
