*Banking Partner Ecosystem Enablement & Orchestration Authority*

## Purpose

Enable and govern integrations between Tyro Banking and external value-add service providers (any partner type), so partners can safely extend or leverage Tyro’s banking capabilities – one use-case we have today through Xero — Initiation of banking workflows (e.g., batch payments, payroll runs, supplier payments).

## Category

Partner Integrations & Value-Add Services Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Consent lifecycle management; Grant/update/revoke consent. Not authoritative for: Account lifecycle, balances, ledger entries → Banking Domain; Transfer execution on rails (NPP/BECS/internal), return codes → Funds Movement Domain.
