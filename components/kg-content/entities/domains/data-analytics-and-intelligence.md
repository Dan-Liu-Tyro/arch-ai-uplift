*Enterprise Data Ingestion, Storage, Productisation & Activation Authority*

## Purpose

Provide Tyro’s technical foundation and operational capability to ingest, transform, store, and serve data as trusted, reusable data products — enabling analytics and cross-domain insights while not owning operational master truth.

## Category

Data & Intelligence Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Data ingestion engineering and operations; Transformation and orchestration tooling operations. Not authoritative for: Operational master truth → Operational Domains; Business decisions → Relevant business domains.
