*Enterprise Data Governance, Assurance & Controls Authority*

## Purpose

Provide Tyro’s enterprise-wide data governance, assurance, and control framework so data across domains is trusted, secure, compliant, and reusable — without owning operational business truth or regulatory policy.

## Category

Data & Intelligence Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Establish enterprise data ownership and stewardship model; Define glossary operating model and semantic lifecycle governance. Not authoritative for: Regulatory interpretation and compliance policy → Risk & Compliance Domains; Authoritative business semantics → Operational Domains.
