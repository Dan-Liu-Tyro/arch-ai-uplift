*Integration Testing, Conformance & Certification Authority*

## Purpose

Provide a consistent enterprise capability to test, certify, and govern external integrations into Tyro across multiple integration surfaces, including — POS integrations; Value-add services integrations; Partners implementing payments via APIs/SDKs.
