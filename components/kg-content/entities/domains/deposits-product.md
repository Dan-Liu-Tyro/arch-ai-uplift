*Regulated Deposit Products Authority (Product Policy + Customer Configuration + Ledger)*

## Purpose

Own Tyro’s regulated banking products and provide the authoritative capability set for deposit products by combining three bounded subcontexts within the Banking domain:

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Define deposit product types and capabilities; Define eligibility and lifecycle policies (policy-level). Not authoritative for: Transfer execution on rails (NPP/BECS/internal) → Funds Movement Domain; Partner consent and external workflow orchestration → Banking VAS Integrations Domain.
