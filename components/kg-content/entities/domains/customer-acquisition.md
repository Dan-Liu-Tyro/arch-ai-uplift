*Go-to-Market Pipeline, Marketing & Sales Execution Authority*

## Purpose

Manage Tyro’s go-to-market engine from market engagement through to a qualified, accepted opportunity and a clean handoff into Customer & Product Onboarding.

## Category

Business Operations Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Marketing campaign execution & optimisation; Campaign lifecycle management, channel execution coordination. Not authoritative for: Customer legal entity master, users, groups/franchises → Customer & Identity; Partner master record & partner lifecycle → Partner Ecosystem.
