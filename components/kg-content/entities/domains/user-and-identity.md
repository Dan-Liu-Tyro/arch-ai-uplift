*System Principals, Authentication & Access Control Metadata Authority*

## Purpose

Provide Tyro’s authoritative capability for managing system identities (principals) and enforcing access through authentication and authorisation controls across customer, partner, and staff channels.

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Identity lifecycle management; Create/update/suspend/remove user accounts and service principals. Not authoritative for: Customer, person, group, partner party master data and relationships → Customer Domain; Business meaning of roles (director, beneficial owner, authorised representative) → Customer Domain.
