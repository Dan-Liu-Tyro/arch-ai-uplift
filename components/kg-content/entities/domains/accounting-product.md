*Merchant Financial Operating System Authority*

## Purpose

Provide merchant-facing financial management capabilities (via Thriday and related services) as the authoritative system for a merchant’s operational accounting records, including — Bookkeeping / journal entries for the merchant; Chart of accounts (merchant-level); Period close and adjustment rules for merchant books.

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Merchant bookkeeping & ledgering; Create and maintain merchant journals. Not authoritative for: Customer legal entity master and identity → Customer & Identity Domain; Merchant acquiring configuration (MID, locations, terminal sharing groups) → Payments Customer Configuration Domain.
