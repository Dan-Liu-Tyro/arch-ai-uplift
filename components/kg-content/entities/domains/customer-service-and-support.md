*Support Interaction, Case & Complaint Authority*

## Purpose

Provide Tyro’s authoritative capability for managing customer support interactions across the customer lifecycle, including — Support cases (incidents, service requests, troubleshooting); Complaints and dispute intake (non-scheme disputes; scheme chargebacks remain in Payments Processing); SLAs, queues, routing, and escalation.

## Category

Support & Experience Channels — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Case & complaint lifecycle management; Create/triage/assign/escalate/resolve/close. Not authoritative for: Customer master, user identity, group hierarchy → Customer & Identity; Merchant configuration (locations, enablement, terminal sharing groups) → Payments Customer Configuration.
