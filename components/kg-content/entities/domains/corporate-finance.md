*Tyro Corporate Books & Statutory Reporting Authority*

## Purpose

Maintain Tyro’s corporate financial books and statutory financial reporting as the authoritative source for — Corporate general ledger (GL) and journal postings; Accounting periods and close processes; Statutory and management financial statements.

## Category

Business Operations Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Corporate journal posting & adjustments; Create/post journals (manual and automated). Not authoritative for: Merchant settlement balances and payout truth → Settlement & Reconciliation Domain; Banking deposit ledger entries and balances → Banking Domain.
