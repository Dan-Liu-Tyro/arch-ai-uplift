*Regulated & Commercial Communications Orchestration Authority*

## Purpose

Decide what communications to send, when, and through which channel, based on events from authoritative domains—ensuring regulated communications are compliant, templates are managed, delivery is tracked, and customer communication logic isn’t duplicated across product systems.

## Category

Cross-Domain Orchestrators — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Communication rules engine (event-to-message orchestration); Channel routing selection (email/SMS/in-app/push/etc.). Not authoritative for: Customer identity and preferences master → Customer & Identity (or dedicated preferences capability, if created); Domain events generation → All authoritative domains.
