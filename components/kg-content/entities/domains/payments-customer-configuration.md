*Merchant Acquiring Configuration Authority (Merchant Setup & Control Plane)*

## Purpose

Maintain the authoritative configuration for how a specific customer is set up to accept payments across Tyro’s acquiring channels (in-store and online). This domain is the “control plane” for acquiring: it defines the merchant’s operational setup that downstream runtime and processing domains must follow.

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Merchant acquiring configuration lifecycle; Create/maintain MerchantID and acquiring setup metadata. Not authoritative for: Customer legal entity master, users, groups/franchises → Customer & Identity Domain; Checkout/session runtime and channel UX → Payments Channels Domain.
