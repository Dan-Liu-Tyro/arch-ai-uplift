*Operational Case Management Capability for Exceptions & Investigations*

## Purpose

A shared and generic enterprise capability for operational case management, investigations, evidence handling, and exception workflow orchestration across multiple domains.

## Category

Cross-Domain Orchestrators — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Case lifecycle, routing, SLA tracking; Evidence management (references, chain-of-custody for evidence metadata). Not authoritative for: Customer-facing support ticketing (if separated) → Customer Service & Support; Transaction/clearing/chargeback truth → Payments Processing.
