*POS Integration Authority*

## Purpose

Define and operate the POS integration surface for Tyro acquiring so POS vendors can initiate and manage payment flows reliably and consistently across supported channels (typically in-store, sometimes omnichannel extensions).

## Category

Partner Integrations & Value-Add Services Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: POS integration API / protocol design (contracts, versions); POS flow orchestration at the integration edge (e.g., request/response choreography). Not authoritative for: not called out as its own section in source — see cached source for embedded notes.
