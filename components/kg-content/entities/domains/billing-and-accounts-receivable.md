*Tyro Customer Billing, Invoicing & Receivables Authority*

## Purpose

Manage what customers owe Tyro by owning the authoritative lifecycle for — Billing accounts and billing profiles (how we bill a customer); Invoice generation and issuance; Accounts receivable (AR) balances, aging, collections state.

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Billing account/profile management (where/ how to bill); Invoice lifecycle (draft → issued → paid/overdue/cancelled). Not authoritative for: Commercial pricing/fee calculation policy → Product Quoting & Pricing Domain; Transaction truth (auth/clearing) → Payments Processing Domain.
