*Payment Rail Execution & Transfer State Authority*

## Purpose

Execute and track the lifecycle of money movement instructions across payment rails, including — BECS/Direct Entry transfers; NPP/PayID/Osko transfers (where applicable); Internal transfers between Tyro accounts.

## Category

Core Customer & Product Domains — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Transfer instruction acceptance and validation (structural correctness, required fields); Rail orchestration and submission (BECS/NPP/internal). Not authoritative for: Regulated deposit ledger and balances → Banking Domain; Settlement batch calculation and payable balances → Settlement & Reconciliation Domain.
