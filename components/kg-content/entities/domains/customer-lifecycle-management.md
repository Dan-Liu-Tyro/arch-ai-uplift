*Post-Activation Lifecycle Orchestration Authority*

## Purpose

Coordinate lifecycle transitions for active customers/products (post-activation), such as suspension, reactivation, upgrades/downgrades, product add-ons, and offboarding. Ensures lifecycle policies are applied consistently across multiple product domains without embedding cross-domain workflow logic inside them.

## Category

Cross-Domain Orchestrators — one of 6 top-level groups; see `docs/domain-model-experiment.md` for the full grouping.

## Authority

Owns: Cross-product lifecycle state machine and orchestration rules; Controlled execution of suspend/reactivate/offboard across products. Not authoritative for: Customer identity truth → Customer & Identity; Merchant configuration truth → Payments Customer Configuration.
